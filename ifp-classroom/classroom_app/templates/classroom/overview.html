How it works

  1. Add each classroom here. Add students and teachers under Students & Teachers (one by one or from a CSV), and give each one or more face photos.
  2. On each Android panel, open that room's kiosk link in Chrome and allow the camera (setup steps: docs/SETUP.md). Android turns the camera off for apps in the background, so keep the kiosk visible: full screen at the start of the class, or in split screen / a floating window beside the lesson.
  3. The panel recognises faces itself and sends "seen" records to this server. Someone's entry is the first time they are seen; their exit is recorded once they have not been seen for the minutes set for that room.
  4. View and download the class register, entry/exit log and monthly register from any PC in the school.
